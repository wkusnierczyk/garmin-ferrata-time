The route as a gauge around the edge, the current difficulty large in the middle. The app's twelfth and thirteenth pages, two versions of one page that differ only in the gauge (owner, 2026-11-21):

- **Page 12:** each section's arc is its length along the route.
- **Page 13:** each section's arc is the metres it climbs: walks and rests shrink, walls take most of the arc.

## Layout

- The **gauge** around the edge, from the lower left over the top to the lower right, each section's arc its length along the route (page 12) or the metres it climbs (page 13).
- Each section at least **3°** of the sweep, the others sharing the rest in proportion (#28): by ascent, the two rests and the two short climbs (Band and Bridge, 5 m up) would otherwise be under a degree. Sections are one degree apart, and the last runs to the route's top, so the gauge ends exactly where the ascent's top dot is. The mock's menu tries none, 2, 3 or 5°.
- The climber as a **thicker orange line** (0.0288 of the screen wide, the sections' own width, round ends; twice the first 0.0144, owner, 2026-11-28, #156; its ends at least 2° inside the gauge's, so the caps stay clear of the ascent numbers) on the gauge's inner side, 0.0072 clear of the sections, over the stretch they could be on (the uncertainty, 25 m up or down). It replaced the first version's orange outline around that stretch, which covered the sections (owner, 2026-11-21); the mock keeps the outline as an option.
- The current difficulty in a large filled box (0.3173 by 0.2212 of the screen) centred on the screen, Bold 0.1683, a rest as `W`, a trapezium narrower at its foot for a section that loses height (#100); everything else placed from it (owner, 2026-11-22).
- Above it, **next** without "in" (Bold 0.0865), its baseline 0.0288 of the screen above the box (owner, 2026-11-28, #157: it was below the box, the altitude above): "C/D 125 m", the grade in its colour, or "Top …" after the last change.
- Below it, the **altitude** (SemiBold 0.0962) with its unit (0.0577), centred on the screen together (owner, 2026-11-23: the number ended at the box's right edge), its capitals' top 0.0288 below the box, the same gap as the line's baseline above it, on pages 3, 4 and 12 to 15 alike (owner, 2026-11-23).
- Under that, the **poi** word (Bold 0.0673, letter-spaced, in its colour), centred, the top of its capitals 0.0288 below the altitude's baseline; only while a point is within the uncertainty, its place left empty otherwise, so nothing moves.
- On a section that **descends**, the order as before: the altitude above the box, next below it, the poi word under next, since what comes next is below (#157). On the **approach** the page is unchanged too: the altitude above, next below.
- At the bottom, the **gap ascent** along the gauge's gap: the start and top dots on the gauge's two ends.
- Difficulty colours: heat with amber, no purple, a colour per half step.
