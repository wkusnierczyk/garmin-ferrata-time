The route as a gauge around the edge, the current difficulty large in the middle. The app's sixth page, in two versions that differ only in the gauge (owner, 2026-11-21):

- **6a:** each section's arc is its length along the route.
- **6b:** each section's arc is the metres it climbs: walks and rests shrink, walls take most of the arc.

## Layout

- The **gauge** around the edge, from the lower left over the top to the lower right, each section's arc its length along the route (6a) or the metres it climbs (6b).
- Each section at least **3°** of the sweep, the others sharing the rest in proportion (#28): by ascent, the two rests and the two short climbs (Band and Bridge, 5 m up) would otherwise be under a degree. Sections are one degree apart, and the last runs to the route's top, so the gauge ends exactly where the ascent's top dot is. The mock's menu tries none, 2, 3 or 5°.
- The climber as a **thicker orange line** (0.0144 of the screen wide, round ends) on the gauge's inner side, 0.0072 clear of the sections, over the stretch they could be on (the uncertainty, 25 m up or down). It replaced the first version's orange outline around that stretch, which covered the sections (owner, 2026-11-21); the mock keeps the outline as an option.
- The current difficulty in a large filled box (0.3173 by 0.2212 of the screen) centred on the screen, Bold 0.1683, a rest as `W`; everything else placed from it (owner, 2026-11-22).
- Above it, 0.0288 of the screen clear of it, the **altitude** (SemiBold 0.0962) with its unit (0.0577), centred on the screen together (owner, 2026-11-23: the number ended at the box's right edge).
- Below it, **next** without "in" (Bold 0.0865), its capitals' top 0.0288 below the box, the same gap as the altitude's baseline above it, on pages 6a to 9 alike (owner, 2026-11-23): "C/D 125 m", the grade in its colour, or "Top …" after the last change.
- Under that, the **poi** word (Bold 0.0673, letter-spaced, in its colour), centred, the top of its capitals 0.0288 below the next line's baseline; only while a point is within the uncertainty, its place left empty otherwise, so nothing moves.
- At the bottom, the **gap ascent** along the gauge's gap: the start and top dots on the gauge's two ends.
- Difficulty colours: heat with amber, no purple, a colour per half step.
