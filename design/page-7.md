The route seen from the front, as on a topo photo of the face. The app's seventh page.

## Layout

- The **front view** on the right, fitted into 184 to 368 px across and 64 to 352 px down, one scale across and up.
- On the left, page 6's middle, smaller: the current difficulty in a filled box (88 by 62 px) centred vertically on the screen; above it the **poi** word and the **altitude** on one baseline, the altitude's number ending at the box's right edge and its unit past it, the word ending a little before the number; below it **next**, on one line.
- Difficulty colours: heat with amber, no purple.

The demo route's sideways travel is made up (`Climb.SIDE`). A real route needs a frontal track traced from a topo; a GPS track gives the map view, not the face.
