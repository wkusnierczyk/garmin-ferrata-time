# Ferrata. Page 2, version 1

Status: settled with the owner on 2026-11-21 (#17)  
Reference mock: `design/demo/page-2.html` (a live dial with a slider and playback, and the options as settings)  
Widgets: `design/widgets.md`

The difficulty widget turned upright, as a variant of the ascent widget: the sections around the climber, and the altitudes where the difficulty changes. The app's second page, after page 1.

## Layout

- The **difficulty column group**, centred on the screen, with altitudes at the column's outer edges too.
- Difficulty colours: heat with amber, no purple (grey, light blue, yellow, amber, orange, red).

## Open

- A rest is flat, so the altitudes at its two borders are equal (in the demo, 1380 above 1380 at the Ledge). Merge the rest into its neighbour, skip one altitude, or keep both.
