Ferrata · pages 4 and 5, version 1

The difficulty column again, now to scale, in two variants: on page 4 each section's box is as tall as the metres it climbs; on page 5, as tall as its length along the route, with the ascent as a gauge around the edge. In the app the box heights would be a setting. The window moves with the climber, who stays a quarter of the way up from its bottom. Altitude ticks on the left, the sections' names on the right. The current box is filled, the others outlined; those behind the climber dimmed.

To look at

  • The window is 288 px tall on the 416 px screen. At 2.5 px per metre it shows about 115 m: about 29 m below the climber and 86 m above.
  • Flat sections (the Ledge and the Bench rests) climb nothing, so they have no height: each is a line across the column at its altitude, with its name.
  • Letters are centred on the part of a box that is in view, and left out when that part is too short for them. Names are centred the same way and cut with an ellipsis to fit the round screen.
  • On page 5 the ascent is a gauge: a thin arc around the edge from the start at the lower left, over the top, to the top of the route at the lower right, with the climber's orange ring at the height climbed. The metres climbed sit just past the start end, the metres left just past the top end, in the gap at the bottom. The stack is centred as a group (tick labels, column, 80 px for names); names stop inside the gauge.
  • By path, flat sections are real boxes, and walls are short. Altitude ticks sit where the route first reaches each altitude: they crowd on walls and spread on walks; a label is left out when it would touch the one below.
  • The boxes meet with no gap. A border between two boxes is drawn in the colour of the one closer to the current box; next to the current box, in the current box's colour.
  • The climber is a white line across the column with the orange ring of the ascent widget on the column's right edge, clear of the letters.
  • The demo plays at a vertical pace: walks pass quickly, walls slowly, and each grade above walking 10 % slower than the one below. A flat section takes as long as 5 m of climbing.
  • Ticks run only along the route, not below its start or above its top. Ticks and labels are in the altitude unit: every 10 m by default, or every 10 ft in feet, which is dense; the step menu changes it. Resolution to be discussed.
  • Not yet: a title above the stack, or the current difficulty and altitude below it, as in the sketch.
