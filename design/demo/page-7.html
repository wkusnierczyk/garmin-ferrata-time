To look at

  • Section 5 descends (owner, 2026-11-23), as in the app's demo route; on the Daubenhorn and the Sulzfluh, which climb there, it is a stand-in descent of its own height, to show how the pages draw one. Every altitude, the profile, the front view and the ascent's ring and numbers follow the real height (down there, and the next section starts lower); the stacks, the gauge by ascent's arcs and the climbed totals count height gained (flat there).
  • The view is from the front: across is the route's sideways travel, up its height, so walks and traverses lie nearly flat and walls stand up. The demo route has no real shape, so its sideways travel is made up, after the style of a topo photo: walks and traverses run nearly flat, walls go nearly straight up with a small wander. A real route would bring its own track.
  • The view is fitted into the right part of the screen (184 to 368 px across, 64 to 352 px down), keeping its shape: one scale across and up.
  • The climber's outline follows the route over the stretch they could be on: on switchbacks it covers several turns.
  • The left column is a widget, the current difficulty group, drawn 50 % larger than its base size, the size of pages 6a/6b, 8 and 9 (owner, 2026-11-23; it was 15 % larger; a menu tries other sizes).
