To look at

  • The view is from the front: across is the route's sideways travel, up its height, so walks and traverses lie nearly flat and walls stand up. The demo route has no real shape, so its sideways travel is made up, after the style of a topo photo: walks and traverses run nearly flat, walls go nearly straight up with a small wander. A real route would bring its own track.
  • The view is fitted into the right part of the screen (184 to 368 px across, 64 to 352 px down), keeping its shape: one scale across and up.
  • The climber's outline follows the route over the stretch they could be on: on switchbacks it covers several turns.
  • The left column is page 6's middle, smaller: the difficulty box; above it the poi word and the altitude on one baseline (the altitude's number ending at the box's right edge, its unit past it); below it what comes next, on one line. Place names, as in the sketch, are left out: the demo route has none.
