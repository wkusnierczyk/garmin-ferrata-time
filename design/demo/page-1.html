To look at

  • Rests are blue rings at the middle of each rest stretch, hollow like the exit rings, in a deeper blue than before so they don't read as green. The two rests in the demo are short (10 and 30 m along), so each ring covers its whole stretch.
  • Danger points are red rings on the route, hollow like the others; the demo has two made up, rockfall below the Dihedral and the exposed Bridge. Their red is the top dot's red, close to K4 in the heat map.
  • Difficulty indicators below the profile: three slots. The larger middle box is the section the climber is estimated in. When the uncertainty stretch reaches past it, the nearest thing behind goes left and the nearest ahead goes right: the neighbouring section, or an exit or danger point inside the stretch. Grades (W, K1 to K5) are filled in the colour map's colours with black letters; rest (R), exit (E) and danger (D) are outlined in their own colours.
  • Four widgets: profile (the route's sections, the rest, exit and danger rings, the climber's outline; the colour map is its option), ascent (the line, its end dots, the climber's ring, the two numbers) difficulty (the middle box and its two satellites, following the profile's symbols and colours) and altitude (the approximate altitude, right-aligned in a box as wide as the route's widest, with its unit). Profile and ascent share the start: the ascent's line rises from the profile's first point, and both tops are level. The profile is 260 px wide on the 416 px screen.
  • The climber's height on the line is an orange ring, 9 px to the middle of its stroke. Its black inside hides the line but not the end dots, and its outline is drawn over everything, so at either end it rings the green or red dot. Each number stays centred on its stretch, but kept 20 px from the ring until it reaches the line's end, where it stops, level with the end. The first and last stills show both cases.
  • The numbers are left of the line, right-aligned 5 px before the climber's ring.
  • The climber's outline is the launcher icon's orange, 3 px wide. Its length is how sure the position is (25 m up or down), so it is short on steep ground and long on flat ground. In the heat map it sits close to K3's orange, and in the one-hue map close to every grade.
  • The colour menu swaps the difficulty colours. The ski piste map reuses green and red, which this page also uses for exits and the line's ends.
  • The option beside the slider marks the line's ends: green at the start, red at the top.
  • The exit rings are hollow, black inside, so the route breaks where an exit leaves, as at the top.
