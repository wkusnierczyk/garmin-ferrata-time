  • "Proportionally" here means equal space: the gap before the ascent, between the two, and after the column are the same, across the screen's width at its middle (56 to 360 px of 416, so the column's altitudes stay inside the round screen).
  • The column as on page 2 (owner, 2026-11-23): one section before the current below it, three after it above; altitudes from the current section's bottom up, the bottom of the section before left out. Empty slots near the start and the top stay empty.
  • The ascent's line runs from the column's bottom edge to its top edge, so the climber's ring and the current box share a height only by chance; they show different things (height climbed against sections around the climber).
  • The column, all five slots, is centred 20 px below the screen's middle to make room for the altitude at the top; the current box sits 48 px below the column's middle (one slot's step).
